## Access, billing, and data

Product and model names, supported surfaces, regional availability, account eligibility, quotas, and plan entitlements change. Check the linked official help page and your account's current limits before relying on a capability. Google AI plans describe consumer app benefits and higher limits; they do **not** mean unlimited access or free production use of the Gemini API. API projects have their own billing, quotas, and terms ([Google AI plans for Mexico](https://one.google.com/intl/es-419_mx/about/google-ai-plans/), [Gemini API billing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/billing/)).

Before uploading code, documents, or Workspace material, check its sensitivity, your organization's rules, the destination product's terms, and the relevant activity/data settings. Google documents that Gemini Apps activity and connected-app data may be used to improve services depending on settings and context ([Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961), [Connected Apps data use](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988)). Do not upload secrets or material you are not authorized to share. For Gemini Notebook, select only sources you are allowed to use and verify its citations against those sources. Google says Gemini Notebook data is not used to train Gemini Notebook unless feedback is provided; feedback may cause the interaction, including uploads, to be reviewed. Qualified Workspace and Workspace for Education users have different terms. Check the current [Gemini Notebook privacy terms](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16164461), and note that interacting with notebooks through Gemini Apps follows Gemini Apps activity settings.
